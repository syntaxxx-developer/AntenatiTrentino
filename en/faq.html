Frequently Asked Questions

How far back can a genealogy research go?

In Trentino, parish registers systematically date back to the Council of Trent (mid-16th century), allowing research to reach up to 1500–1600 in most parishes.

What details do I need to provide to start?

Providing the full name of an ancestor born in Trentino, an approximate date of birth or emigration, and if possible the original village or valley is ideal.
