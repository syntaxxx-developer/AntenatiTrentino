Sources for genealogical research in Trentino-Alto Adige: a historical and archival overview

Author: Serena Baldessari | Published on Traces - Ancestors in Trentino

Genealogy research in Trentino-Alto Adige presents unique characteristics compared to other Italian regions, owing to the complex administrative and religious history of the territory.

1. Parish Registers

The primary source consists of parish records. Following Council of Trent directives (1545-1563), priests systematically logged baptisms and marriages, followed by death records and Status Animarum.

2. Civil Status Records

Under Habsburg administration and later transition to Italy, military draft records at the State Archive of Trento offer key details on physical traits and enlistment.
